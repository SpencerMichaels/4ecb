# Legacy sheet fields not on the modern sheet

Survey of 2026-11-06: every field and tracker of the legacy sheet viewer
(`reverse-engineering/generated/decompiled/CharacterSheetViewer/CharTracker/`,
ignored by git) and the official fillable character sheet, compared with the
modern printed sheet. Save counts are from `sample-chars/*.dnd4e`. Owner rule:
what the legacy sheet tracks, the modern sheet tracks too, unless the owner
decides otherwise below.

## Play-mode (M6) candidates

Live tracker state; pencil versions already exist on the scratchpad.
`CUR_HitPoints`, `CUR_TempHP`, `CUR_Surges`, `CUR_SecWind`,
`CUR_DeathSave1-3` (`PanelModelHitPoints.cs`), `CUR_Action Points`
(`PanelModelActionPoints.cs`), `CUR_Milestones` (milestone also grants +1 AP),
`USAGE_<power>`/`USAGE_<item>` used flags, and the Short rest / Extended rest /
Milestone buttons (`CharacterSheetDisplay.cs:1944-2062`: a short rest clears
encounter uses, Second Wind and temp HP; an extended rest also clears dailies,
item uses and milestones, resets HP, surges and death saves, and sets AP to
base).
